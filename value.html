Lower score = better value.

#HotelValue score≈ per pointVerdict

Value score = points per night ÷ cash price per night. A lower number means you're spending fewer points for each £ of room — so the lower, the better. As a rough guide: under 150 is excellent, up to 250 is good, up to 350 is fair, and above that the cash rate is usually the smarter buy.
